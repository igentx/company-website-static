## Web Development & AI

**Q: What makes IGENTX a strong choice for web development?**
A: We combine AI-driven development, modern frameworks like Next.js, and SEO-first design to build fast, secure, and scalable websites that perform across search engines and devices.

**Q: How much does website development cost?**
A: Every project is scoped individually based on complexity, integrations, languages, and timeline. Contact sales at https://www.igentx.com/contact or hello@igentx.com for a tailored proposal.

**Q: How long does it take to develop a website?**
A: Standard websites take 1–3 weeks. Ecommerce or complex sites can take 4–6 weeks. AI-accelerated projects often complete in 2–4 weeks.

**Q: What is included in a web development project?**
A: Typical deliverables include UI/UX design, SEO setup, analytics integration, responsive design, CMS integration (Storyblok/WordPress/Sanity), performance optimisation, hosting setup, and launch support. Contact sales to scope your project.

**Q: Do you offer SEO-optimized websites?**
A: Yes — all IGENTX sites are SEO-first with schema, metadata, Core Web Vitals optimisation, and structured data.

**Q: What technologies do you use?**
A: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, Vercel, Storyblok, TailwindCSS — PWA-ready and accessible by default.

**Q: Can you redesign an old website?**
A: Yes. We modernize outdated sites, improving design, speed, UX, and SEO while preserving brand identity.

**Q: Do you provide ecommerce development?**
A: Yes — Shopify, WooCommerce, headless commerce (custom APIs), and performance-first ecommerce builds.

**Q: Is my website mobile-friendly?**
A: Every IGENTX website is responsive for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

**Q: Do you include CMS features?**
A: Yes — Storyblok (recommended), WordPress, or custom dashboards are available for easy content edits.

**Q: Will my website include Google Analytics?**
A: Yes — GA4, Search Console and Meta Pixel integrations are standard.

**Q: What makes IGENTX AI-powered web development faster?**
A: AI automates repetitive coding, layout scaffolding, optimisation and testing — reducing manual work and speeding delivery.

**Q: How does AI improve SEO & performance?**
A: AI analyses user behavior, speed metrics and search trends to improve Core Web Vitals, metadata relevance, and indexing.

**Q: What industries do you serve?**
A: Retail, ecommerce, hospitality, real estate, healthcare, education, and technology — globally, with deep UAE experience.

**Q: Does IGENTX support Arabic and English?**
A: Yes — full bilingual (RTL for Arabic) support including SEO tags for both languages.

**Q: Can you migrate my existing website?**
A: Yes — we migrate WordPress, Shopify, Magento, and custom sites to a modern stack with zero downtime.

**Q: Do you provide ecommerce in the UAE?**
A: Yes — UAE ecommerce with Arabic support and compliant checkout systems.

**Q: How secure are IGENTX websites?**
A: Security includes SSL encryption, server hardening, automated security audits and regular patching.

**Q: Do you provide maintenance & hosting?**
A: Yes — Vercel hosting, monitoring, backups and ongoing maintenance scoped per project.

**Q: What support after launch?**
A: Post-launch support is defined in your project proposal. Contact sales to discuss ongoing support or SEO retainers.

**Q: Payment methods?**
