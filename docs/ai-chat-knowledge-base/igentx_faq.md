**Q: Do you integrate UAE payment gateways?**
A: Yes. PayTabs, Telr, Stripe, Checkout.com and other UAE-compliant gateways.

**Q: Shopify vs BigCommerce: which is right for my business?**
A: Shopify excels for fast setup, app ecosystem and smaller-to-mid catalogues. BigCommerce suits larger catalogues, B2B features and headless commerce flexibility. We assess your needs and may recommend a headless Next.js storefront for maximum performance.

**Q: What is Headless Commerce?**
A: Headless commerce separates the storefront (frontend) from the commerce engine (backend) via APIs. Enables blazing-fast custom storefronts, omnichannel experiences and design freedom while keeping robust cart, inventory and payment logic.
