## Platform Capabilities

### Admissions
Magic-link onboarding. Configurable admission form builder with per-program templates and conditional fields, parent magic-link onboarding, staff fill-on-behalf, enrollment fee summary emails, and one-click approval into student records and first invoices.

### Leads Pipeline
Capture website enquiries, review optional AI chat conversations, and convert qualified families into admissions without a separate CRM. **Staff manually convert leads to the pipeline.**

### Attendance
Daily check-in and check-out, QR scan check-in, leave requests with staff approval, and a real-time picture of who is in for admins and families.

### Activity Feed
Teachers log meals, naps, learning, mood and milestones in seconds. Optional **Write with AI** drafts from a short note and/or photos; staff review before posting. Photo attachments reach families through private storage, scoped to authorised parents only.

### Messaging
1:1 conversations between parents, teachers and admins, plus broadcasts for closures and reminders. Staff can draft DMs and broadcasts with Write with AI, then review and send. Realtime inbox updates and email notifications when configured.

### AI Translation (Included)
Built-in AI translation for multilingual families. **Included at no extra cost**: unlimited for normal daycare use.

- **On-demand Translate** on messages, broadcasts, and activity feed updates
- **100+ languages:** full ISO 639-1 catalog with 20 popular choices listed first (Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam, Tamil, German, Spanish, and more)
- **Searchable picker:** find any supported language without contacting support
- **Preferred translation language** saved on parent and staff profiles for one-tap translate
- **Server-side processing**: API keys stay on the server, not in the browser
- **Product page:** https://www.clayox.com/ai-translation

**Do not claim:** 150+ languages, auto-translate on every message, or per-message fees for normal centre usage. Say "100+ languages" or "full ISO language list".

### Write with AI (Optional / env-gated)
Staff drafts for activities (note and/or up to 4 photos), progress summaries, staff notes, DMs, and broadcasts. **Never auto-posts or auto-sends.** Parents never see Write with AI; they use Translate only.

- **Product page:** https://www.clayox.com/ai-write-childcare-updates
- **IGENTX blog:** https://www.igentx.com/blog/ai-write-clayox-staff-drafts

**Do not claim:** auto-posting, inventing details not in notes/photos, assessment frameworks, or structured learning journals.

### Billing
Invoice-first. Reusable fee plans, billing runs, parent payment proof workflow, credit notes and account statements with tax frozen at issue time. Fee estimator with branded PDF. Online card payment gateways are **not implemented**.

### Student Lifecycle
Manage withdrawal with retention policies, archives, re-enrollment for returning families and program promotion to the next classroom. Printable **student ID cards** with custom templates and single or bulk PDF generation.

### Admin Tools
Fee estimator PDF, plus helpers for age/program eligibility, classroom occupancy snapshots, and compliance CSV exports.

### Help Center
Role-specific in-app guides for admins, teachers, and parents.

### Embeddable Enquiry Forms
Drop enquiry forms into your existing centre website with consent capture. Leads land in your admin board for review and one-click admission conversion.

**Important:** This is enquiry and lead capture only. There is no hosted visit booking page.

### White-label Branding
Your centre name, logo and favicon on your dedicated deployment. Parents and staff see your brand in the portals they use daily, and invoice PDFs carry your centre's branding.

Approved line: "Your centre's branding on your dedicated deployment." Do not claim complete vendor-brand removal.

### Optional AI Enquiry Assistant
Deployable for subscribing centres. Captures conversations for admin review. **Manual lead conversion only:** nothing is auto-logged without staff action. Chat inquiries appear at **Leads → Chat inquiries**.

**Distinct from AI Translation and Write with AI:** Translation is for reading existing updates in another language. Write with AI is for staff English drafts. The enquiry assistant is an optional website chat add-on for lead capture.

## How It Works

1. **Capture Enquiries** (Day one): Embed enquiry forms, use the hosted register page, or deploy the optional AI enquiry assistant for subscribers.
2. **Enrol Families** (Minutes): Send magic-link admission invites, collect documents from parents, approve enrolments into student records and first invoices.
3. **Run Daily Operations** (Every day): Track attendance, log activities with optional Write with AI drafts, keep families updated through the activity feed and messaging.
4. **Bill and Grow** (Ongoing): Run billing cycles, record payments, review payment proofs, issue statements and manage the full student lifecycle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Clayox only for UAE centres?**
A: No. Clayox is built for early childhood education providers worldwide. Currency, timezone and tax/VAT are configurable per deployment. The UAE is our primary market, not the geographic limit of the product.

**Q: Who is Clayox for?**
A: Preschools, nurseries, childcare centres, early learning centres, Montessori schools, kindergartens and playschools that need admissions, daily care, family communication and billing in one platform.

**Q: How does magic-link admission onboarding work?**
A: Admins send a one-shot magic-link invite to a parent's email. Parents complete configurable admission forms at their own pace. Staff can fill forms on behalf of families, email an enrollment fee summary, then approve in one click to create student records and first invoices.

**Q: Can we embed enquiry forms on our existing website?**
A: Yes. Embeddable enquiry forms for lead capture on your centre website, plus a hosted register page alternative. This is enquiry and lead capture, not a hosted visit booking page.

**Q: Can teachers log attendance and activities from mobile?**
A: Yes. Teacher and family portals are responsive and mobile-ready, with PWA add-to-homescreen support.

**Q: Does billing support tax and VAT configuration?**
A: Yes. Configurable currency, timezone and tax/VAT settings per deployment. Tax rates frozen on each invoice at issue time.

**Q: Is each centre on its own deployment?**
A: Yes. Each centre runs on a dedicated single-tenant deployment with its own database, private storage and CDN.

**Q: What does white-label mean for Clayox?**
A: The software runs under your centre's brand. Your centre name, logo and favicon appear on your dedicated deployment, and invoice PDFs carry your branding.

**Q: What roles and permissions are supported?**
A: 8-role role-based access control with granular permissions across Admin, Teacher and Family portals.

**Q: Is AI translation included in Clayox?**
A: Yes. AI translation is included for normal daycare use. Parents and staff tap Translate on messages, broadcasts, and activity updates. See https://www.clayox.com/ai-translation

**Q: How many languages does Clayox translation support?**
A: 100+ languages from the full ISO 639-1 catalog. Twenty popular languages for UAE and international nurseries are listed first; users search for the rest. Users save a preferred language on their profile. See https://www.clayox.com/ai-translation

**Q: What is Write with AI, and does it auto-post?**
A: Write with AI drafts staff content for activities, progress summaries, staff notes, DMs, and broadcasts. It never auto-posts or auto-sends. Staff review before anything reaches families. Parents do not see Write with AI. See https://www.clayox.com/ai-write-childcare-updates and https://www.igentx.com/blog/ai-write-clayox-staff-drafts

**Q: Do you support online card payment gateways?**
A: Not yet. Clayox supports invoice-first billing with parent payment proof submissions and manual payment recording. Automated card gateways are on the roadmap.

**Q: Can we print student ID cards?**
A: Yes. Admins can design custom ID card templates and generate single-card or bulk print-ready PDFs.

**Q: Does the AI enquiry assistant auto-create leads?**
A: No. The optional AI enquiry assistant captures conversations for admin review. Your team manually converts qualified enquiries to the leads pipeline.

**Q: Is the AI enquiry assistant included for all centres?**
A: Optional and deployable for subscribing centres. IGENTX can also help implement the AI Customer Service Agent on your centre website.

**Q: Can parents view daily activities and message teachers?**
A: Yes. The Family portal provides a per-child activity feed and 1:1 messaging with teachers and admins.

**Q: How do I get started with Clayox?**
A: Visit clayox.com to explore and request a demo. For implementation support or combining Clayox with website and AI enquiry solutions, talk to the IGENTX team at https://www.igentx.com/contact
