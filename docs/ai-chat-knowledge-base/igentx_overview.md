## Delivery Process

Seven-step process from discovery through continuous growth:

1. **Discovery** (3–5 days) — Goals, audience, constraints and success metrics
2. **Strategy** (3–5 days) — Scope, architecture, SEO approach and delivery roadmap
3. **UX & Design** (1–2 weeks) — Wireframes, visual design and prototypes
4. **Development** (2–6 weeks) — Modern stacks, integrations and performance best practices
5. **QA & Optimisation** (3–7 days) — Testing, accessibility, Core Web Vitals and security
6. **Launch** (1–3 days) — Deployment, analytics, handover and go-live support
7. **Continuous Growth** (Ongoing) — SEO, content, feature iterations and monitoring
